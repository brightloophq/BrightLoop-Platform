# Auxion Product Bible

## What this is

The **Auxion Product Bible** is the permanent, canonical source of truth for the Auxion product.
It defines what Auxion is, why it exists, how it thinks, how it is built, and where it is going.
Every chapter captures a durable product decision — not a temporary implementation detail — so that
the intent behind the product survives across people, tools, and time.

## Who should use it

- **Product & strategy** — to set and defend direction.
- **Designers** (and Claude Design) — to ground every interface decision in the product's principles and standards.
- **Developers** (and Claude Code) — to build against the product's architecture, data model, and engineering standards.
- **Strategists and operators** — to understand the vision, personas, journeys, and roadmap.
- **Future AI agents** — to act on behalf of the product with full, authoritative context.

## It is a living document

This Bible is expected to evolve. Chapters move from `Draft` to reviewed and are updated as the
product matures. Changes are deliberate and recorded — see `00-cover-and-version.md` for the
version and change history.

## Mandatory reference

**Claude Code, Claude Design, developers, designers, strategists, and future AI agents must
reference this Product Bible** before making product, design, or implementation decisions. It is
the shared context that keeps every contributor — human or AI — aligned.

## The prime rule

**No implementation decision may contradict this Product Bible without an explicit, documented
update to it first.** If reality needs to diverge from what is written here, change the Bible in
the same motion — update the relevant chapter, note it in the version history, and only then build.
A silent contradiction between the product and the Bible is a defect.
